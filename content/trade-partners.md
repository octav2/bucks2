## The Trade Problem We Solve

### Stop Worrying About Wi-Fi Callbacks & Data Cabling Hassles

When building or renovating high-specification properties, network infrastructure is often an afterthought—until the client moves in and the Wi-Fi drops.

* **For Electricians:** Data cabling requires specialized testing equipment, rack termination, and network configuration that eats into your high-voltage AC schedule.
* **For Main Contractors & Builders:** Poor wireless coverage or bad cable termination leads to client complaints after handover, delaying final project sign-off and retentions.
* **For Garden Room Builders:** Clients expect seamless Gigabit internet the day their garden studio is built, but standard Wi-Fi extenders fail over long distances and through insulated walls.
