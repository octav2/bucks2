## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why hire professional CCTV installers instead of fitting wireless battery cameras?
Hardwired PoE CCTV cameras do not rely on batteries or wireless signals. They record continuously 24/7 in true 4K resolution directly to a local NVR hard drive inside your property. Wireless battery cameras only record short clips when triggered, frequently miss key events, suffer signal interference, and demand ongoing monthly cloud subscription fees.

### Can I view my CCTV security cameras on my phone when away from home?
Yes. You get free encrypted access to your camera feeds through the UniFi Protect app on iOS and Android. You can view live video, playback past events, receive AI motion alerts, and download video clips directly to your phone without subscription charges.

### What happens to the CCTV system if my internet connection goes down?
Your security cameras continue recording 24/7 without interruption because they are hardwired directly to the local NVR on your property. Internet is only required when you want to view live video feeds remotely on your mobile phone.

### Do you install smart door access control and video intercom systems?
Yes. We install smart door access control systems, video doorbells, and commercial intercoms that integrate directly into the same mobile app as your security cameras, allowing you to verify visitors and unlock doors remotely.

### How many days of video footage are stored on the NVR recorder?
Our standard NVR storage configurations hold between 14 to 30 days of continuous 24/7 4K footage across 4 to 8 cameras. Once the drive reaches capacity, it automatically overwrites the oldest footage — all stored locally with zero monthly cloud fees.
