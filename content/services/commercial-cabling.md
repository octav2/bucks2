## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you carry out office cabling work outside normal business hours?
Yes. We offer weekend and evening installation slots so your team experiences zero downtime or noisy drilling during office working hours.

### What is the difference between Cat6 and Cat6a cabling?
Standard Cat6 supports up to 1Gbps speeds over 100m (and 10Gbps up to 37–55m). Cat6a is fully shielded and supports 10Gbps speeds over the full 100m distance. We recommend Cat6a for high-bandwidth commercial environments, video editing suites, or future-proofing.

### Do you supply testing documentation upon completion?
Yes. Every single cable run is tested for wire-map continuity and speed performance. We provide a clearly labelled patch diagram matching wall sockets to patch panel ports.

### Can you fix or re-tidy an existing messy server cabinet?
Absolutely. We offer data rack re-dressing and audit services—tracing existing chaotic cables, replacing oversized patch leads with custom lengths, and clearly labelling all ports.
