# CCTV Installation in Great Missenden

Great Missenden's rural and village properties can need security coverage across a wider footprint than the front and rear doors alone. Larger plots may include long driveways, side entrances, garages, workshops, garden buildings and other approach routes that sit well beyond the strongest indoor Wi-Fi signal.

Bucks Tech Help installs **hardwired 4K IP CCTV** using the UniFi Protect ecosystem. Cameras connect over Ethernet and PoE to a local NVR, giving the property a wired recording system without making a monthly cloud-storage subscription part of the core setup.

[Explore our smart security service](/services/smart-security) or [view the Great Missenden services](/locations/great-missenden).

## 4K CCTV Without the Monthly Subscription

Wireless cameras can look convenient when only one entrance needs coverage, but a larger property can expose their weaknesses quickly. Cameras near a gate, garage or outbuilding may sit outside reliable Wi-Fi coverage, and cloud-dependent systems can introduce ongoing storage fees.

A wired PoE system uses Ethernet for both data and power. Footage is stored on an on-site UniFi Protect NVR, while remote viewing and notifications can still be configured through the Protect app.

A Great Missenden installation can include:

- 4K-capable UniFi Protect IP cameras;
- hardwired Cat6 or Cat6a PoE connections;
- local NVR recording;
- compatible human and vehicle detection;
- coverage for entrances, driveways, garages and outbuildings;
- smartphone live view and playback;
- compatible video doorbells and intercoms;
- smart door-access options;
- integration with an existing or new UniFi network.

The camera layout starts with the actual approach routes and vulnerable areas rather than a pre-selected camera count.

## CCTV for Rural Homes and Larger Plots

On a larger HP16 property, useful security positions can be spread around the site.

A survey can consider:

- driveway entrances;
- front and rear doors;
- pedestrian gates;
- garages and car ports;
- side access;
- workshops or stores;
- garden offices and detached buildings.

Hardwired PoE is valuable in those locations because the camera receives a direct connection back to the network and recorder. It does not have to rely on a wireless signal reaching the far corner of the property.

## Security for Period and Listed Homes

Character properties often need more care around cable routes and camera placement.

The installation should aim for useful views without making equipment or cabling unnecessarily prominent. Suitable lofts, service spaces and discreet external routes can be considered where practical, while listed-building constraints may require additional care or permissions.

The result should feel planned for the property rather than added as a visible consumer-camera patchwork.

## Smart Door Access and Video Entry

Compatible UniFi access-control and video-entry hardware can be considered where a property needs managed entry as well as cameras.

Depending on the entrance and network, that may include suitable intercom, video-entry or access-control devices within the wider UniFi ecosystem.

Because gates and older entrances vary significantly, access-control hardware should be scoped around the actual location, cable route and power requirements rather than offered as a generic add-on.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does CCTV installation cost in Great Missenden?

Bucks Tech Help currently lists 4K IP CCTV projects from £1,800. The final price depends on camera count, cable routes, recorder capacity, coverage requirements and any access-control hardware. A site survey or quote audit is used to scope the system.

### Is there a monthly subscription fee for CCTV?

The core UniFi Protect systems are designed around local NVR storage, so there is no required monthly cloud-storage subscription for recording footage. Remote viewing can be configured through the UniFi Protect app.

### Can CCTV cover a long driveway and outbuildings?

Yes. A wired PoE design can place cameras around entrances, driveways, garages and outbuildings without asking each camera to depend on weak Wi-Fi at the edge of the property.

### Do you install commercial security systems in Great Missenden?

Great Missenden is primarily treated here as a residential and rural market. Bucks Tech Help can still assess suitable local business premises, but this page focuses on homes, larger plots and outbuildings rather than forcing a commercial security angle.
