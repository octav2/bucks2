## Cabling to Garden Offices

Garden offices are one of the most obvious local reasons to install a permanent wired backbone.

If the workspace sits 20 or 30 metres from the house, relying on a mesh node still means relying on wireless signal crossing the garden and another external wall.

Where a suitable route is practical, an external or armoured Ethernet link can extend the main network to the studio. The far end can then support:

- a local Wi-Fi access point;
- wired workstations;
- a small PoE switch;
- a printer or VoIP device;
- compatible CCTV equipment.

## Cabling for Loft Offices and Extensions

Loft conversions and extensions can change the network layout without moving the broadband point.

Planning a dedicated Ethernet run means the new space can receive its own access point or wired outlets rather than relying on the original router to reach through several floors or into the rear of the property.

If cabling is considered during renovation work, routes can often be installed more discreetly before final finishes are complete.
