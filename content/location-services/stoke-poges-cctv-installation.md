## 4K CCTV Without the Monthly Subscription

Wireless cameras can be convenient for one doorway, but large residential plots expose the compromises quickly. Each camera depends on a stable wireless connection and often a cloud-storage plan, even if it sits near a gate or detached garage.

A hardwired PoE system uses Ethernet for both power and data. Footage records to an on-site UniFi Protect NVR, while remote viewing and compatible alerts can still be configured through the Protect app.

A Stoke Poges installation can include:

- 4K-capable UniFi Protect IP cameras;
- hardwired Cat6 or Cat6a PoE connections;
- local NVR recording;
- compatible person and vehicle detection;
- entrance and driveway coverage;
- garage, annex and outbuilding cameras;
- smartphone live view and playback;
- compatible video intercoms;
- smart gate or door-access options;
- integration with an existing or new UniFi network.

The system is designed around the site's actual approach routes and lines of sight rather than a fixed camera bundle.

## CCTV for Gated Entrances and Driveways

A private entrance can be one of the most important camera positions on a larger Stoke Poges property, and one of the furthest from the house.

Where site conditions allow, an appropriate external or armoured network route can extend connectivity toward the gate. That provides a wired foundation for a camera or compatible intercom system rather than relying on the house Wi-Fi reaching the entrance.

A survey can consider:

- vehicle gates;
- pedestrian entrances;
- long driveways;
- garages and car ports;
- side access;
- annexes and workshops;
- other suitable perimeter positions.

## Security for Larger Detached Properties

On a larger SL2 plot, useful camera positions may be spread around several buildings.

A wired PoE backbone allows cameras at the house, garage, gate and other suitable locations to connect back to the same network and recorder. That makes the security system part of the wider property infrastructure instead of a collection of standalone wireless devices.

Local NVR recording also keeps the core footage storage at the property rather than making paid cloud storage a requirement.

## Smart Gate and Door Access

Compatible UniFi intercom and access-control hardware can be considered where the property needs managed entry alongside CCTV.

Depending on the entrance, that may include suitable gate intercom, video-entry or door-access equipment within the wider UniFi ecosystem.

Because electric gates vary in distance, power and automation hardware, the correct design should be scoped around the actual entrance rather than treated as a standard add-on.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does CCTV installation cost in Stoke Poges?

Bucks Tech Help currently lists 4K IP CCTV projects from £1,800. The final price depends on camera count, cable routes, recorder capacity, driveway or gate coverage and any access-control hardware. A site survey or quote audit is used to scope the system.

### Is there a monthly subscription fee for CCTV?

The core UniFi Protect systems are designed around local NVR storage, so there is no required monthly cloud-storage subscription for recording footage. Remote viewing can be configured through the UniFi Protect app.

### Can CCTV cover a long driveway and electric entrance gate?

Yes. A hardwired PoE design can extend camera connectivity toward gates, driveways, garages and outbuildings using appropriate cable routes, avoiding dependence on weak Wi-Fi at the edge of a large property.

### Can you add smart gate or door access alongside CCTV?

Yes. Compatible UniFi intercom, door-entry and access-control hardware can be considered alongside a suitable UniFi Protect and network design, depending on the entrance, gate equipment and cabling requirements.
