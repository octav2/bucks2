# CCTV Installation in Gerrards Cross

Gerrards Cross properties often need security coverage across more than a front door and driveway. Larger detached homes and generous plots can include gates, garages, side access, garden buildings and several approach routes, so a useful CCTV system needs to be planned around the whole site.

Bucks Tech Help installs **hardwired 4K IP CCTV** using the UniFi Protect ecosystem. Cameras connect over Ethernet and record to a local NVR, giving you an owned system without making paid cloud storage part of the core recording setup.

[Explore our smart security service](/services/smart-security) or [view the Gerrards Cross services](/locations/gerrards-cross).

## 4K CCTV Without the Monthly Subscription

Wireless battery cameras can look simple at first, but on a larger property they create several points of dependency: battery condition, Wi-Fi signal at each camera location, cloud connectivity and often a recurring storage subscription.

A wired PoE system takes a different approach. Ethernet carries both data and power to the camera, while footage is stored on an on-site UniFi Protect NVR. Remote viewing and notifications can still be configured through the UniFi Protect app, but the recording system itself remains at the property.

A Gerrards Cross installation can include:

- 4K-capable UniFi Protect IP cameras;
- hardwired Cat6 or Cat6a PoE connections;
- local NVR recording;
- compatible human and vehicle detection features;
- coverage for gates, entrances, garages, driveways and outbuildings;
- smartphone live view and playback;
- compatible video doorbells and intercoms;
- smart door-access options;
- integration with an existing or new UniFi network.

The objective is not to install the largest camera package. It is to position the right cameras around the areas that need useful coverage.

## CCTV for Large Gerrards Cross Properties

Larger SL9 homes often have more perimeter to think about than a typical suburban plot. A front entrance may be only one part of the security picture.

A survey can consider:

- vehicle entrances and longer driveways;
- pedestrian gates;
- front, side and rear doors;
- garages and car ports;
- garden rooms and detached offices;
- side passages and service access;
- outbuildings and storage areas.

Hardwired PoE is particularly valuable at the edge of the property because each camera has a direct network connection instead of depending on whatever Wi-Fi happens to reach that corner of the site.

## Subscription-Free Local Recording

UniFi Protect is designed around local recording. Footage is stored on an NVR installed at the property rather than requiring every recording to be uploaded to a paid third-party cloud service.

That gives the homeowner direct control of the recording hardware while still allowing remote access through the Protect app when required.

For larger systems, storage capacity can be selected around the number of cameras and the required retention period rather than tied to a cloud subscription tier.

## Smart Door Access and Video Entry

Where security extends beyond cameras, compatible UniFi access-control and video-entry hardware can be integrated with the wider network.

That can be useful at pedestrian gates, main entrances or controlled internal doors where the property needs a combination of visual verification and managed access.

Because every entrance is different, access-control hardware should be scoped around the door, gate, power availability and cable route rather than added as a generic package.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does CCTV installation cost in Gerrards Cross?

Bucks Tech Help currently lists 4K IP CCTV projects from £1,800. The final price depends on camera count, cable routes, recorder capacity, coverage requirements and any access-control hardware. A site survey or quote audit is used to scope the system.

### Is there a monthly subscription fee for CCTV?

The core UniFi Protect systems are designed around local NVR storage, so there is no required monthly cloud-storage subscription for recording footage. Remote viewing can be configured through the UniFi Protect app.

### Can CCTV cover a large driveway and outbuildings?

Yes. A wired PoE design can place cameras around entrances, driveways, garages and outbuildings without asking each camera to depend on weak Wi-Fi at the edge of the property.

### Can you add smart door access alongside CCTV?

Yes. Compatible UniFi door-entry, intercom and access-control hardware can be integrated with a suitable UniFi Protect and network setup, depending on the entrance and cabling requirements.
