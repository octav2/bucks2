Berkhamsted's security requirements span period homes, larger family properties and a genuinely active town-centre business market. ## 4K CCTV Without a Required Cloud-Storage Subscription

Wireless cameras can be convenient for simple coverage, but they still depend on a stable wireless connection and often a recurring cloud-storage plan.

A hardwired PoE system uses Ethernet for both power and data. Footage records to an on-site UniFi Protect NVR, while remote viewing and compatible alerts can still be configured through the Protect app.

A Berkhamsted installation can include:

- 4K-capable UniFi Protect IP cameras;
- hardwired Cat6 or Cat6a PoE connections;
- local NVR recording;
- compatible person and vehicle detection;
- entrance, driveway and side-access coverage;
- garage and garden-building cameras;
- smartphone live view and playback;
- compatible video doorbells and intercoms;
- smart door-access options;
- integration with an existing or new UniFi network.

The camera plan starts with useful lines of sight rather than a fixed bundle size.

## CCTV for Historic and Period Homes

Period properties need camera and cable routes to be visually considered.

The objective is useful security coverage without making cabling or hardware unnecessarily prominent on the building. Suitable loft spaces, service routes and discreet external paths can be used where practical.

A survey can consider:

- front and rear entrances;
- side passages;
- driveways;
- garages;
- garden rooms;
- other suitable access routes.

Listed or particularly sensitive properties may require additional care and any relevant permissions before work affecting protected fabric.

## CCTV for Larger Family Homes

On a larger HP4 property, cameras may need to cover several parts of the plot.

Hardwired PoE gives each camera a direct path back to the network and recorder rather than relying on Wi-Fi reaching the edge of the property.

That can be especially useful for cameras positioned near garages, gates or garden buildings.

Local NVR storage also means the core recording system remains at the property instead of requiring footage to be stored behind a recurring third-party cloud plan.
