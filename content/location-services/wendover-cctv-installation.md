## 4K CCTV Without the Monthly Subscription

Wireless consumer cameras can be convenient for basic coverage, but every camera still depends on wireless signal and often a recurring cloud-storage plan.

That becomes less attractive when a camera sits near a garage, driveway or garden building where indoor Wi-Fi is already weaker.

A hardwired PoE system uses Ethernet for both data and power. Footage records to an on-site UniFi Protect NVR, while remote viewing and compatible alerts can still be configured through the Protect app.

A Wendover installation can include:

- 4K-capable UniFi Protect IP cameras;
- hardwired Cat6 or Cat6a PoE connections;
- local NVR recording;
- compatible person and vehicle detection;
- entrance and driveway coverage;
- garage and garden-building cameras;
- smartphone live view and playback;
- compatible video doorbells and intercoms;
- smart door-access options;
- integration with an existing or new UniFi network.

The camera plan starts with the actual approach routes and lines of sight rather than a fixed bundle size.

## CCTV for Larger Family Homes

On a larger HP22 property, cameras may need to cover several parts of the plot.

Hardwired PoE gives each camera a direct path back to the recorder instead of depending on whatever Wi-Fi reaches the edge of the property.

That can make a significant difference for cameras mounted near garages, side gates or detached buildings.

Local NVR storage also means the core recording system remains at the property rather than requiring a paid third-party cloud plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does CCTV installation cost in Wendover?

Bucks Tech Help currently lists 4K IP CCTV projects from £1,800. The final price depends on camera count, cable routes, recorder capacity, coverage requirements and any access-control hardware. A site survey or quote audit is used to scope the system.

### Is there a monthly subscription fee for CCTV?

The core UniFi Protect systems are designed around local NVR storage, so there is no required monthly cloud-storage subscription for recording footage. Remote viewing can be configured through the UniFi Protect app.

### Can CCTV cover driveways, garages and garden buildings?

Yes. A hardwired PoE design can place cameras around entrances, driveways, garages and outbuildings without making each camera depend on Wi-Fi at the edge of the property.

### Do you install CCTV for Wendover businesses?

Yes. Bucks Tech Help can scope hardwired IP CCTV and supporting network infrastructure for suitable local shops, offices, clinics and other small commercial premises as well as homes.
