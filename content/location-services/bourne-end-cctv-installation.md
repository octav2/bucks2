## 4K CCTV Without the Monthly Subscription

Wireless cameras can be convenient for simple coverage, but larger plots expose the compromises quickly. Each camera depends on wireless signal, a power source and often a cloud-storage plan, even when it sits at the far end of a garden or beside an outbuilding.

A hardwired PoE system uses Ethernet for both power and data. Footage is stored on an on-site UniFi Protect NVR, while remote viewing and alerts can still be configured through the Protect app.

A Bourne End installation can include:

- 4K-capable UniFi Protect IP cameras;
- hardwired Cat6 or Cat6a PoE connections;
- local NVR recording;
- compatible human and vehicle detection;
- coverage for entrances, driveways, gardens and outbuildings;
- suitable river-deck or perimeter positions;
- smartphone live view and playback;
- compatible video doorbells and intercoms;
- smart door-access options;
- integration with an existing or new UniFi network.

The system is planned around the actual access points and lines of sight rather than a fixed camera bundle.

## Subscription-Free Local Recording

UniFi Protect records to local NVR hardware installed at the property.

That means the core recording system is owned outright rather than requiring footage to be stored behind a recurring third-party cloud plan. Remote viewing can still be available through the Protect app, but the recording infrastructure remains on-site.

For a larger camera system, the NVR and storage capacity can be scoped around the number of cameras and required retention rather than chosen from a cloud subscription tier.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does CCTV installation cost in Bourne End?

Bucks Tech Help currently lists 4K IP CCTV projects from £1,800. The final price depends on camera count, cable routes, recorder capacity, coverage requirements and any access-control hardware. A site survey or quote audit is used to scope the system.

### Is there a monthly subscription fee for CCTV?

The core UniFi Protect systems are designed around local NVR storage, so there is no required monthly cloud-storage subscription for recording footage. Remote viewing can be configured through the UniFi Protect app.

### Can CCTV cover gardens, river decks and outbuildings?

Yes. A hardwired PoE design can place cameras around entrances, gardens, outbuildings and other suitable perimeter areas without asking each camera to depend on Wi-Fi at the edge of the property.

### Can you add smart door access alongside CCTV?

Yes. Compatible UniFi door-entry, intercom and access-control hardware can be integrated with a suitable UniFi Protect and network setup, depending on the entrance and cabling requirements.
