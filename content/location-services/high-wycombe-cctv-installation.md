## 4K CCTV Without the Monthly Subscription

Wireless consumer cameras can be useful for simple coverage, but larger homes and business premises quickly expose their limitations. Each camera still depends on power, a stable wireless connection and often a cloud-storage plan.

A hardwired PoE system uses Ethernet for both power and data. Cameras record continuously to a local UniFi Protect NVR, while remote viewing and alerts can still be configured through the Protect app.

A High Wycombe installation can include:

- 4K-capable UniFi Protect IP cameras;
- hardwired Cat6 or Cat6a PoE cabling;
- local NVR storage;
- compatible human and vehicle detection;
- cameras for entrances, driveways, car parks and loading areas;
- smartphone live view and playback;
- compatible video doorbells and intercoms;
- smart door-access options;
- integration with an existing or new UniFi network.

The system is designed around the areas that need useful visibility, not around a pre-selected camera bundle.
