## Cabling in Georgian, Victorian and Listed Properties

Older Berkhamsted homes can make cable routing the defining part of the project.

The shortest route may not be appropriate if it creates unnecessary disruption to historic finishes. Depending on the building, suitable routes can include loft spaces, floor voids, cellar or service areas and discreet external sections.

The network should reach the necessary rooms while keeping the visible installation restrained.

For listed or sensitive properties, any work affecting protected fabric may require additional care or permission.

## Cabling for Loft Offices and Multi-Storey Homes

Vertical homes need vertical network planning.

A loft office may need its own access point and wired desk connection, while lower floors support TVs, smart-home devices and cameras.

Dedicated Cat6 or Cat6a runs let each floor connect back to the central network over cable rather than asking devices to share a wireless backhaul between storeys.
