# CCTV Installation in Chesham

Chesham properties range from closely spaced Victorian terraces near the centre to modern estates and more rural homes around the edges of HP5. That means useful CCTV coverage can involve very different layouts: a compact front-and-rear access plan in one property, or a longer driveway, side gate and detached building in another.

Bucks Tech Help installs **hardwired 4K IP CCTV** using the UniFi Protect ecosystem. Cameras are connected over Ethernet and recorded to a local NVR, so the core recording system does not depend on a paid cloud-storage subscription.

[Explore our smart security service](/services/smart-security) or [view the Chesham services hub](/locations/chesham).

## 4K CCTV Without the Monthly Subscription

Consumer wireless cameras are convenient to place, but each camera still needs power, dependable Wi-Fi and somewhere to store footage. At the edge of a property or behind the same walls that already weaken indoor wireless coverage, that can turn a security device into another unreliable Wi-Fi client.

A hardwired PoE system uses Ethernet for both data and power. Footage can be recorded continuously to an on-site UniFi Protect NVR, while remote viewing and notifications can be configured through the UniFi Protect app.

A Chesham installation can include:

- 4K-capable UniFi Protect IP cameras;
- hardwired Cat6 or Cat6a PoE connections;
- local NVR recording;
- compatible human and vehicle detection features;
- coverage for entrances, passages, driveways, garages and outbuildings;
- smartphone live view and playback;
- compatible video doorbell or smart-access options;
- integration with an existing or new UniFi network.

The camera plan should follow the actual approach routes and access points around the property rather than starting from a pre-set bundle size.

## Residential CCTV for Chesham Homes

Terraced and town-centre properties often have fewer external elevations, but the useful views still need careful positioning. Front approaches, side alleys, rear gates and garden buildings can all matter, while camera placement should avoid creating unnecessary blind spots or relying on a distant wireless connection.

Larger or more rural HP5 properties can require broader coverage across:

- driveways and vehicle entrances;
- front and rear doors;
- side access and passageways;
- garages and workshops;
- detached garden offices;
- outbuildings and other approach points.

Hardwired PoE is particularly useful when those cameras sit outside reliable house Wi-Fi coverage.

## Smart Door Access and UniFi Protect

Compatible UniFi door-entry and access-control hardware can be considered where a property needs more than cameras alone.

For an existing UniFi environment, bringing Wi-Fi, CCTV and compatible access devices into the same wider ecosystem can reduce the number of separate platforms used to manage the property.

The right door-access hardware depends on the entrance, cabling route and required level of control, so it should be scoped as part of the site survey.
