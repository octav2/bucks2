# Wi-Fi Installation in Marlow

Marlow combines period riverside properties, multi-storey townhouses, newer developments and larger detached homes with patios, gardens and outbuildings that people increasingly expect to be part of the same connected property. In those layouts, the broadband router may be several floors or solid walls away from an upstairs bedroom, outdoor entertaining area or garden workspace.

Bucks Tech Help installs **hardwired Ubiquiti UniFi networks** across SL7, using Ethernet backhaul to feed access points where the Wi-Fi is actually needed. Instead of relying on wireless mesh nodes to relay signal through the property, the network is distributed over cable first and Wi-Fi handles the shorter final connection to devices.

## Why Marlow Homes Need Hardwired Wi-Fi, Not Extenders

Multi-storey homes and period properties can be difficult environments for wireless backhaul. A mesh node placed on an upper floor still needs a strong connection back to another node, and concrete slabs, dense walls or long internal distances can weaken that path before your phone or laptop even joins the network.

A hardwired design avoids using Wi-Fi for the backbone wherever practical. Cat6 or Cat6a cabling feeds strategically positioned UniFi access points so each floor, zone or outdoor area has a nearby wireless source.

This is particularly useful for:

- upper-floor **Wi-Fi dead zones** in multi-storey townhouses;
- riverside or period homes with dense construction;
- home offices where video calls need a consistent connection;
- large patios, outdoor kitchens or entertaining areas;
- garden offices and detached studios;
- media rooms, smart-home systems and fixed AV equipment;
- properties where outdoor cameras and access devices also share the network.

Where the device mix and wider network justify it, Bucks Tech Help can also design around current UniFi **Wi-Fi 7 installation** hardware.

## What We Install

A Marlow whole-home Wi-Fi project can include:

- Ubiquiti UniFi access points positioned around the actual floor plan;
- solid-copper Cat6 or Cat6a Ethernet backhaul;

In multi-storey and character properties, cable routes can be planned through service risers, floor voids, loft spaces and discreet external paths to minimise visible cabling.

## Outdoor Wi-Fi for Patios and Riverside Gardens

Marlow's riverside setting makes outdoor connectivity more relevant than it is on a typical suburban page. A patio, outdoor dining area or garden should not have to rely on a signal leaking through several internal walls and an external wall from the nearest indoor access point.

Where appropriate, an outdoor-rated access point can be connected directly back to the wired network and positioned to serve the external area itself.

That creates a much cleaner design for:

- riverside patios;
- outdoor kitchens;
- larger gardens;
- terraces and entertaining areas;
- garden studios;
- spaces close to a mooring or river-facing boundary.

The network is delivered outdoors deliberately rather than treated as leftover indoor coverage.

## Wi-Fi for Garden Buildings and Outbuildings

A detached building presents the same problem in another form. If the connection to a garden office, studio or other outbuilding depends entirely on a wireless hop from the house, performance can change with distance, construction and foliage.

Where a cable route is practical, Bucks Tech Help can extend the main network to the outbuilding and provide Wi-Fi locally at the far end. The building can then have its own access point, switch or wired desk connection while remaining part of the same managed network.
