# CCTV Installation in Marlow

Marlow's security requirements can be as varied as its buildings. Period riverside homes may need discreet camera routes around entrances and side access, while larger detached properties can require coverage across driveways, garages, patios and garden buildings. In the town centre, restaurants, shops, offices and hospitality premises have a genuine need for dependable commercial security infrastructure as well.

Bucks Tech Help installs **hardwired 4K IP CCTV** using the UniFi Protect ecosystem, with cameras connected over Ethernet and footage recorded to a local NVR. The core recording system stays on-site rather than depending on a required monthly cloud-storage subscription.

[Explore our smart security service](/services/smart-security) or [view the Marlow services hub](/locations/marlow).

## 4K CCTV Without the Monthly Subscription

Wireless consumer cameras can be easy to mount, but they still depend on wireless coverage, power and often a recurring cloud-storage plan. Those dependencies become more noticeable when cameras sit around the edge of a larger property or in a business environment where reliable recording matters.

A hardwired PoE design uses Ethernet for both data and power. Footage is stored locally on a UniFi Protect NVR, while remote viewing and notifications can still be configured through the Protect app.

A Marlow installation can include:

- 4K-capable UniFi Protect IP cameras;
- hardwired Cat6 or Cat6a PoE connections;
- local NVR recording;
- compatible human and vehicle detection;
- coverage for doors, side access, driveways, patios and outbuildings;
- smartphone live view and playback;
- compatible video doorbells and intercoms;
- smart door-access options;
- integration with an existing or new UniFi network.

The design starts with the areas that genuinely need useful visibility rather than a fixed camera count.

## Residential CCTV for Marlow Homes

For riverside and larger residential properties, a camera plan can consider more than the obvious front-door view.

Depending on the layout, useful positions can include:

- main and secondary entrances;
- side gates and passages;
- driveways and garages;
- rear doors and patio approaches;
- garden rooms and detached offices;
- outbuildings and storage areas.

Hardwired PoE is particularly useful where those camera positions sit beyond dependable indoor Wi-Fi coverage.

## Commercial CCTV for Marlow Businesses

Marlow's commercial angle is strong enough to deserve its own section. The town has retail, hospitality and professional premises where **commercial security services** can be designed around the actual operation.

Suitable installations can support:

- restaurants and hospitality venues;
- shops and retail premises;
- offices and studios;
- reception areas and customer entrances;
- back-of-house or stock areas;
- staff entrances and service routes.

Where the premises also need guest Wi-Fi, EPOS connectivity or new structured cabling, the CCTV system can share the same managed PoE backbone and rack infrastructure.

## Smart Door Access and Video Entry

Compatible UniFi access-control and video-entry hardware can be integrated where a property needs managed access alongside CCTV.

That can include suitable door-entry, intercom or controlled-access hardware within the wider UniFi ecosystem.

The correct design depends on the entrance, cabling route and access requirement, so it should be scoped during the survey rather than added as a generic package.
