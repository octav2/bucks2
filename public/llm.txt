# Bucks Tech Help - Business Information

## About Us
Bucks Tech Help is a local IT support and computer technician service based in Buckinghamshire, UK. We specialize in patient, jargon-free home technology support. Our primary audience includes older people, adult children helping their parents, and busy professionals who need home tech support without the stress.
