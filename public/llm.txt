# Bucks Tech Help - Business Information

## About Us
Bucks Tech Help is a local network & cabling infrastructure agency based in Buckinghamshire, UK.
We design, install and test enterprise Wi-Fi, structured Cat6/Cat6a cabling and IP CCTV solutions
for large homes, garden offices, estates/farms and businesses. We sell high-ticket infrastructure
projects, typically £1,500–£4,000+, not micro-fix services.

## Core Services
* Whole-Property & Garden Room Wi-Fi: Hardwired Ubiquiti UniFi and TP-Link Omada access points with zero dead zones.
* Commercial Cat6/Cat6a Structured Cabling: Office cabling, patch panels, racks and managed switch installations.
* IP CCTV & Access Control: Commercial-grade 4K IP cameras, on-site NVR storage, subscription-free (no monthly fees).

## Service Areas (Buckinghamshire)
Beaconsfield, Amersham, Chesham, Gerrards Cross, High Wycombe, and surrounding towns across South & Central Buckinghamshire.
