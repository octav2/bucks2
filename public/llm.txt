## Technical Specifications & Engineering Standards
- Hardware Platforms: Ubiquiti UniFi (U7 Pro, UniFi Protect), TP-Link Omada Enterprise, Managed PoE/PoE+ Switches.
- Cabling Standards: 100% Solid Copper Cat6 and Cat6a (no cheap CCA), Steel Wire Armored (SWA) for underground outbuildings, Fluke DTX certified performance mapping.
- Architecture Specialties: Non-invasive cable routing through thick Chiltern flint walls, solid brick period homes, listed estates, loft conversions, and modern foil-insulated extensions.
