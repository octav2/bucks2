# Bucks Tech Help

Friendly, patient, jargon-free home technology support across Buckinghamshire.

## Summary
Bucks Tech Help provides professional home technology support for residents across Buckinghamshire. We specialize in helping people who find technology frustrating, providing patient and clear assistance without technical jargon.

## Core Services
- **Wi-Fi & Connectivity**: Troubleshooting dead zones, router setup, and signal improvement.
- **Printers & Peripherals**: Setup, troubleshooting, and wireless printing configuration.
- **Mobile Devices**: Help with iPhones, iPads, Android phones, and tablets.
- **Smart Home & Entertainment**: Smart TV setup, streaming services (Netflix, iPlayer), and voice assistants.
- **Security & Support**: Email issues, scam message protection, and general software troubleshooting.
- **Tech MOT**: A comprehensive service to update, secure, and optimize all home devices.
