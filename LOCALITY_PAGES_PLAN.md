# Locality Pages Plan

## Purpose

Locality pages can help the website rank for service searches in specific Buckinghamshire towns.

These pages should only be created once the main homepage is live and the business is ready to invest in SEO.

Do not create lots of thin, duplicated pages.

Each locality page should be useful, specific and locally relevant.

## Priority towns

Start with the most valuable and relevant areas:

1. Beaconsfield
2. Amersham
3. High Wycombe
4. Marlow
5. Gerrards Cross
6. Chesham
7. Aylesbury
8. Chalfont St Peter

## Location page structure

Each location page should follow this structure:

# Bucks Tech Help in [Town]

Intro paragraph:
Friendly, patient home technology support in [Town] for Wi-Fi problems, printers, phones, tablets, laptops, smart TVs, email issues and scam messages.

## Local Home Tech Support in [Town]

Explain the service in that town. Mention common customer needs and why people locally may use the service.

## How I Can Help in [Town]

Include service sections:

- Wi-Fi help
- Printer help
- Phone and tablet setup
- Laptop and computer help
- Smart TV and streaming setup
- Scam email and online safety checks
- Tech help for seniors

## Common Problems I Help With

Examples:

- Wi-Fi does not reach upstairs
- Printer says offline
- New iPad setup
- Smart TV apps not working
- Email password issues
- Suspicious scam messages
- Slow laptop
- Help for elderly parents

## Nearby Areas Covered

Mention nearby villages and neighbourhoods relevant to the town.

## Pricing

Include standard pricing.

## How It Works

1. Get in touch
2. Explain the problem
3. Book a visit
4. I visit and help
5. Follow-up if needed

## FAQ

Add local FAQ.

## Contact CTA

Need home tech help in [Town]? Call or WhatsApp to check availability.
