# Design Brief

## Overall style

The website should feel:

- Friendly
- Local
- Trustworthy
- Calm
- Clear
- Professional but not corporate
- Suitable for older visitors
- Easy to read on mobile

Avoid a dark, overly technical or futuristic design.

Do not make the site look like a corporate IT company or a gadget repair shop.

The site should feel like a helpful local service.

## Colour direction

Suggested colour palette:

- Deep blue or navy for trust
- Soft light blue backgrounds
- White sections
- Light grey borders
- Green accent for reassurance or success
- Warm accent colour for buttons if needed

Example feel:

- Trustworthy blue
- Clean white
- Soft grey
- Friendly green highlights

## Typography

Use large, readable text.

Important for older users:

- Body text should not be too small
- Buttons should be large and easy to tap
- Good line spacing
- High contrast
- Avoid thin grey text

## Layout

The homepage should be structured like this:

1. Header with logo/name, phone, WhatsApp button
2. Hero section with clear headline and call to action
3. Trust bar: Local, patient, jargon-free, evening/weekend appointments
4. Services cards
5. Who I help
6. No drilling/no wiring note
7. Pricing
8. Digital MOT package
9. How it works
10. Areas covered
11. FAQ
12. Contact section
13. Footer

## Header

Header should include:

- Business name/logo
- Services anchor link
- Pricing anchor link
- Areas anchor link
- FAQ anchor link
- Contact anchor link
- Call button
- WhatsApp button

On mobile, use a simple hamburger menu or keep header minimal.

## Hero section

Hero should include:

- Strong headline
- Short supporting paragraph
- Primary CTA: Book a Home Visit
- Secondary CTA: WhatsApp Me
- Optional trust text: Local Buckinghamshire service. Evening and weekend appointments.

Suggested hero visual:

- Friendly photo of the owner
- Or simple illustration/photo of laptop, phone, router and tablet
- Avoid stock photos that look fake

## CTA buttons

Primary CTA:

- Book a Home Visit

Secondary CTA:

- WhatsApp Me

Button style should be large, clear and mobile-friendly.

## Accessibility

The website should be accessible and easy to use.

Requirements:

- Good colour contrast
- Large buttons
- Semantic HTML
- Clear headings
- Descriptive links
- Mobile responsive
- No tiny text
- No excessive animations
- Contact details visible without hunting

## Imagery

Use real photos where possible.

Useful photos:

- Friendly photo of the owner
- Laptop and phone on a table
- Router or mesh Wi-Fi device
- Smart TV remote
- Printer setup
- Small toolkit/cables

Avoid showing:

- Customer private details
- Passwords
- Emails
- Personal documents
- Bank screens
