# Project Brief

## Business concept

The business is a local home tech support service in Buckinghamshire.

It helps homeowners, older people, busy families and adult children supporting elderly parents with everyday technology problems.

The tone should be friendly, calm, local, professional and jargon-free.

The business should feel like a trustworthy local person, not a large corporate IT company.

## Target customers

Main target customers:

1. Older people who struggle with technology.
2. Adult children looking for help for parents or grandparents.
3. Busy professionals who do not have time to troubleshoot devices.
4. Homeowners with Wi-Fi, printer, laptop, phone, iPad or smart TV issues.
5. People worried about scam emails, suspicious texts or account security.

## Customer pain points

Customers may say things like:

- My Wi-Fi does not reach the whole house.
- My printer will not connect.
- I bought a new iPad and do not know how to set it up.
- My smart TV apps are confusing.
- I cannot access my email.
- I think I clicked on a scam message.
- I need someone patient to explain this properly.
- My parents need help with their phone and tablet.
- I do not want to call a big company or wait on hold.

## Main promise

Patient, local, jargon-free home technology help across Buckinghamshire.

## Important boundaries

The website must clearly explain that the business focuses on setup, support and troubleshooting.

The business does not carry out drilling, hardwiring, electrical work, cabling through walls or wall mounting.

## Conversion actions

The website should encourage users to:

- Call
- WhatsApp
- Send an enquiry
- Book a home visit

WhatsApp should be prominent because many customers and family members may prefer quick messaging.

## Trust signals

Use the following trust signals where true:

- Local Buckinghamshire service
- DBS checked
- Fully insured
- Patient and jargon-free
- Evening and weekend appointments
- Clear pricing
- No hard selling
- No unnecessary upselling
- Respectful with passwords and personal information
- Home visits by appointment

Do not include DBS checked or insured unless this is actually true.
