## FAQ

### Do you repair broken phones or laptop hardware?

No. I do not repair cracked screens, broken laptop parts or internal hardware faults. I focus on setup, troubleshooting and everyday home tech support.

### Do you do drilling, cabling or wall mounting?

No. I do not drill holes, mount TVs, run cables through walls or carry out electrical work.

I can help with the technical setup of plug-in devices, apps, Wi-Fi and accounts.

### Can you help elderly parents?

Yes. This is one of the main reasons people contact me. I provide patient, clear and respectful support for older people who need help using technology confidently.

### Can you help with scam emails or suspicious texts?

Yes. I can help check suspicious messages, explain what to look for, improve account security and advise on next steps. If money or bank details are involved, I will always recommend contacting your bank directly.

### Can you guarantee every problem will be fixed?

No one can honestly guarantee that every issue can be fixed, especially if a device is very old, damaged, locked or dependent on a provider such as BT, Sky, Apple, Microsoft or Google.

However, I will always do my best to diagnose the issue and give honest advice.

### Do I need to know my passwords?

For many jobs, yes. If you need help with email, Apple ID, Google, Netflix, Wi-Fi or Microsoft accounts, you may need access to your passwords or recovery phone/email.

I can guide you through recovery if needed.

### Do you offer remote support?

For some simple issues, yes. However, many Wi-Fi, printer and setup problems are easier to solve in person.

### What areas do you cover?

I cover Buckinghamshire and nearby areas. Contact me with your postcode and I will let you know if I can visit.
